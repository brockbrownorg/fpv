The Flying

Three Ways to Fly

No clients, no deadlines, just a hobby that keeps finding new skies over Nebraska and Kansas.

Freestyle and Flow

Gaps, dives and power loops at parks and open fields around Omaha. The kind of flying where the line matters more than the destination, and every pack teaches something new.

Chases

FPV chases at the drag strip and the motocross track, and the occasional Cessna on final approach. Speed, timing and a front row seat that only a small drone can get.

Golden Hour

Slow sunset flights over lakes, rivers and small towns in Nebraska and Kansas. The quiet end of the day, filmed from a few hundred feet up.

Where I Fly

Omaha, Nebraska

Home base. Weekend flights across the Omaha metro, including Bellevue, Papillion, La Vista and Council Bluffs, plus lakes, parks and small towns across eastern Nebraska.

Wichita, Kansas

Second home turf. Flying in Wichita and south central Kansas, from Goddard and Derby to the Arkansas River trails, with roots in the Kansas flying community.
